About

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, studying while employed. Research

Knowledge editing for LLMs
2026 – present · Research Intern (remote), University of Tennessee, advised by Tianci Liu

Unstructured editors can reproduce an editing passage yet fail to recall its individual facts once the passage is gone. Later facts see richer context during editing, start at a lower loss, and look easier to learn than they are. FOVEATED randomly shifts the RoPE positions of preceding-context keys during editing only, so each sentence is learned from a focused view.

→ 5 editors · 2 LLM backbones · 3 benchmarks
Reliability of retrieval-augmented generation
2026 – present · independent research, while employed

When one false claim is copied or paraphrased across many nominal sources, agreement-based reliability scoring counts the copies as independent votes. Effective-Source Reliability merges near-duplicate sources first, so a laundered claim counts once. A related study shows that AI-generated rewrites keep their ranking while hiding where the evidence came from.

→ source laundering · false consensus · source lineage
